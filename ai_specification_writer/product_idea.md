# Product Idea - Carpool Connect

## Vision
Enable commuters to share rides efficiently, reducing daily transportation costs, traffic congestion, and carbon emissions through a seamlessly integrated mobile platform.

## Users
- **Drivers**: Want to fill empty seats during their daily commute and share fuel/maintenance costs.
- **Passengers**: Want affordable, safe, and reliable rides without relying on expensive taxi services or crowded public transport.

## Key Features
- **Real-time Ride Matching**: Instant matching between drivers and passengers with overlapping routes.
- **In-App Payment Integration**: Automated and secure split-payment system for seamless transactions.
- **Ratings and User Reviews**: Verification system and user feedback to build community trust and safety.
- **Eco-Impact Tracking**: Personal analytics tracking carbon footprint reduction and money saved over time.
