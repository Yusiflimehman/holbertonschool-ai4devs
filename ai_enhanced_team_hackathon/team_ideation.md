# Team Ideation

## Brainstormed Ideas
1. **AI Study Planner** – generates a personalized weekly study schedule from a student's courses, deadlines and available time.
2. **AI Code Review Buddy** – reviews a pasted code snippet and explains bugs, style issues and improvements in simple language.
3. **AI Meeting Notes Summarizer** – turns raw meeting transcripts into summaries, decisions and action items. ✅ (chosen)

## Problem Statement
Teams spend a lot of time in meetings, but important decisions and action items are often lost because nobody writes clear notes. Writing and sharing summaries manually is slow and inconsistent. An AI tool can turn a raw transcript into a structured summary with decisions and action items in seconds.

## MVP Features
- Paste or upload a meeting transcript (text)
- AI generates a short summary of the meeting
- AI extracts key decisions and action items (with owner and deadline when mentioned)
- User can edit the result and copy/export it as Markdown
- Simple history page showing previously summarized meetings
